Implicit Surface is a simple Java application that lets you visualize surfaces of the form:

S={(x,y,z)∈R3:f(x,y,z)=0}

where f:R3→R. A popular example is the sphere, which is defined as:

x2+y2+z2−1=0

Or a double torus:

r=x2(1−x2)−y2r2+z2−0.04=0

There are many other surfaces to explore here. Check out the demo in the video below. Enjoy!
