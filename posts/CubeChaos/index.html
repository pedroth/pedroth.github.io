Cube Chaos is a simple application where you can see several particles being driven by force or velocity vector fields. It is called chaos because one of the vector fields is the Lorenz attractor from chaos theory.

Feel free to download the program below.

Lorenz Attractor system

x˙=σ(y−x)y˙​=x(ρ−z)−yz˙=xy−βz​​​

Here, σ, ρ, and β are free parameters of the model.
