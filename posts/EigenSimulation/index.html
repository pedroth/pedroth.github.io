Eigen Simulation is a Java application that lets you visualize three algorithms for finding real eigenvectors.

For starters, an eigenvector is a vector with the following property:

Let A∈Rn×n be a matrix and v∈Rn be a vector. Then v is a non-trivial solution (that is, v=0) of the equation below.

Av=λv(1)

These eigenvectors and eigenvalues (the λ values in (1)) are essential in Mathematics and very useful in fields such as Physics, Optimization, and Machine Learning. Here I explore three algorithms that compute a special kind of eigenvectors called real eigenvectors. For these eigenvectors, the matrix in (1) must satisfy A=AT. These matrices are called symmetric matrices, and they guarantee the existence of real eigenvectors and eigenvalues. The first method is the power method, and the other two are based on a constrained optimization problem:

x∈Rnmin​ or x∈Rnmax​⟨x,Ax⟩

such that ∣x∣2=1(2)

To visualize these algorithms, the application draws a sphere (the constraint space in (2)) painted with colors. The color represents the value of the cost function in (2). Brighter colors mean higher cost, while darker colors mean lower cost.

In the application, you can press the 3 key to see the gradient vector field of this cost function on the sphere. The video below shows a demo of this program.
